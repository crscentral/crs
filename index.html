About Section

The Revenue Gap No One Talks About

Here is a truth that surprises most hotel owners: big hotel chains do not earn more simply because they have more rooms. They earn more because they have dedicated revenue management teams working every day to ensure the right room is sold at the right price, through the right channel, to the right guest.

Independent hotels rarely have access to that kind of expertise. Until now. CRS Central was built specifically to close that gap. Our senior team comes from five-star international hotel operations, with decades of hands-on experience in revenue strategy, OTA management, dynamic pricing, and yield management.

We take that same institutional knowledge and make it accessible — and affordable — for independent properties in Thailand, Laos, India, Nepal, Malaysia and Vietnam. When you partner with us, you are not hiring a vendor. You are gaining a dedicated revenue partner who is as invested in your hotel's growth as you are.

About CRS Central & Core Services

How do you help Hotels?

We maximize revenue and reduce costs — by managing pricing, OTA channels, reservations, and guest communications more effectively than most hotels can do with their existing in-house resources.

How is this different from hiring my own in-house Revenue Manager?

A full-time in-house revenue manager typically costs significantly more in salary alone, before software, training, or turnover risk. CRS Central gives you the same (often higher) level of expertise, backed by a full team instead of one person, at a fraction of the cost — with no recruitment, no training period, and no gap in coverage if someone leaves.

What is included in CRS Central's Hotel Revenue Management Services?

Our comprehensive service includes:

  • OTA Management
  • Inventory Management
  • Rate Management
  • Dynamic Pricing & Yield Optimization
  • Market & Competitor Intelligence
  • Forecasting & Demand Analysis
  • Promotions & Campaign Management
  • Daily Performance Reporting
  • Daily suggested walk-in rates sent directly to your team
  • A full support team on standby for any assistance you may need

Pricing & Packaging

What are the charges for Hotel Revenue Management?

Our pricing starts from USD 300 per month and goes up to USD 2,800 per month. The final price depends entirely on the number of rooms in your hotel. There are no hidden costs, no commissions, and no additional taxes—just one flat, transparent fee.

Do you take a commission on top of the monthly fee?

No. Our pricing is a single flat monthly fee with no commission on your bookings or revenue, no setup fees, and no hidden costs.

What are the charges for a PMS & Channel Manager?

Our Property Management System (PMS) and Channel Manager services start at USD 55 per month.

What are the charges for hotel website development, and what is required?

Website development starts at a one-time charge of USD 380. All you need to do is provide high-quality photos and descriptions of your services, and we will build a fully functional website for your hotel.

What are the charges for Digital Marketing, and what does it include?

Our digital marketing service starts from USD 299/month, covering 15 posts, 2 videos, and weekly Meta Ads setup across Instagram, Facebook & YouTube. Higher-volume content plans or additional platforms are available at a custom rate.

Can I combine services like Revenue Management, PMS, and Digital Marketing?

Yes — most hotels combine two or more services since they work best together (e.g., Revenue Management + PMS keeps pricing and inventory perfectly in sync). Bundled pricing is available — ask your revenue audit specialist for a combined quote.
